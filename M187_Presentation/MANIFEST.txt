THREE KEY IMPROVEMENTS (highlight as callout boxes):

1. BACKGROUND SUBTRACTION (Fabrini et al.)
   PC = density_condensed / density_dilute
   (where densities are computed after subtracting minimum voxel intensity)
   → Largest single accuracy jump (2.04 → 3.33 PC)

2. CONNECTED-COMPONENT NUCLEI CLEANUP
   Cellpose 76 instance labels → 3D connected components → 5 true nuclei
   → Removes internal fragments from condensate texture interference
   → Cleans up dilute-phase pixel selection

3. ROBUST DILUTE-PHASE DENSITY
   Find all ~88,000 valid 10×10×10 patches
   Sort by intensity, average the lowest 50
   → Eliminates seed-dependent PC swing (4.3 to 6.0 → stable 6.297)
